## Per-file purpose

The dated packet carries the complete Northern America evidence, assignment
replacements, source records and pins, polity sheet, assertions, and accepted
review digest. The generator deterministically rebuilds that packet from the
accepted baseline and tracked Natural Earth country fabric. The packet index,
todo, roadmap, and history advance M25C to eleven promoted regions. The
certification test fixes packet counts, the review digest, removed generic
scaffold actor, and required date-valid actors. This manifest records the exact
shipping boundary and its validation evidence.

## User-goal mapping

Northern America is risk-first because its 3,986 assignments make it the
largest remaining region and 3,258 provisional assignments collapsed the
pre-Columbian continent into one generic actor. The replacement pins exact
validity to `1444-11-11`, hashes complete source records plus exact locators,
and supplies four gap-free Grade-A rows for geometry, politics, hierarchy, and
gazetteer relationships.

The sheet distinguishes late Norse Greenland, Thule and ancestral Inuit,
subarctic, Pacific, Plains, southwestern, Mississippian, Iroquoian, Eastern
Woodlands, and uninhabited-island fabrics. It deliberately avoids fabricated
hard frontiers and does not project later contact-era tribal maps backward.

## Residual risk

The accepted M23 fabric and surviving evidence cannot support defensible exact
local borders for most 1444 North American communities. The packet therefore
uses explicit coarse political fabrics with 0.35 uncertainty, checked centers,
and no hard boundary. These groupings are evidence-bounded replacements for a
generic actor, not claims of uniform sovereignty or immutable culture areas.
